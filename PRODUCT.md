# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Gallery visitors, contemporary-art audiences, collectors, companies, educators, and schools who want to discover Kogo's programme, artists, artworks, and services or contact the gallery.

## Product Purpose

Present Kogo Gallery's programme and artists with clarity and cultural confidence, make current exhibitions and artworks easy to discover, and turn interest into visits, enquiries, and participation.

## Brand Personality

Cultured, direct, approachable. The site should feel like a confident contemporary gallery: visually led, editorially precise, and welcoming without becoming decorative or corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic agency cards, decorative gradients, stock SaaS layouts, excessive motion, and ornamental styling that competes with the artwork. Do not soften the established monochrome grid or replace its direct language with marketing filler.

## Design Principles

- Let artwork and exhibition photography carry the page.
- Preserve the strict grid, ruled sections, and purposeful asymmetry already used across the site.
- Keep copy compact, specific, and easy to scan.
- Reuse established native WordPress block patterns before introducing new components.
- Make every action and image work across keyboard, reduced-motion, and small-screen contexts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic headings, descriptive image alternatives, visible focus treatment, usable touch targets, readable line lengths, and the existing reduced-motion behavior. Do not communicate meaning through color alone.
